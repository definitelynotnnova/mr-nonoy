Mr. Nonoy Privacy Policy

Mr. Nonoy is the community bot for the Roblox game Bahay Kubo Garden and its Discord server. This page explains what the bot stores about you, why, for how long, and how to have it deleted.

What we store

The bot only stores what its features need. It never stores the text of your messages.

FeatureWhat is stored
LevelsYour Discord user ID, your XP total, the time of your last message that earned XP, and whether you used /level optout.
Support ticketsTicket number, channel ID, your Discord user ID, the staff member handling it, timestamps, and the Roblox username or ID you type into the ticket form. Ticket messages and attachments stay in Discord.
Account linksA cache that links a Discord user ID to a Roblox user ID, looked up through Bloxlink when staff need it.
Game moderationRecords from the game's anti-cheat: Roblox user ID, username, account age, the server you played on and what was detected. Staff notes and actions on those records include the staff member's Discord user ID.
Server settingsChannel and role IDs that the server admins configure. These are not personal data.

Message content

In the channels the admins enable for XP, the bot reads a new message's text for a moment to decide whether it should earn XP. It strips mentions, emojis and links, checks that enough real text is left, and compares a short fingerprint with your previous message so repeated spam does not count.

  • The text is processed in memory and discarded. It is never written to disk, logged or shared.
  • The fingerprint of your previous message lives only in memory and is lost when the bot restarts.
  • You can stop this at any time with /level optout. The bot then ignores your messages before reading any text. /level optin turns it back on.
  • Message content is never used to train machine learning or AI models.

Why we use it

  • To greet new members and give back earned level roles when someone rejoins.
  • To run the level system and its leaderboard.
  • To run support tickets for ban appeals, player reports and help.
  • To let staff moderate cheating in the game and in the Discord server.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build profiles for any purpose other than moderation of our own game and server.

Who we share it with

  • Discord, which runs the platform the bot works on.
  • Roblox, through its Open Cloud API, to read game data and apply or lift game bans.
  • Bloxlink, which receives a Discord or Roblox user ID when staff look up a linked account.

Within the server, only staff can see moderation details. Nobody else receives your data.

How it is protected

  • All data the bot stores on disk is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept apart from the data.
  • The bot runs on a private server that is only reachable over SSH with key authentication, behind a firewall.
  • Daily backups are encrypted in the same way.

How long we keep it

DataKept for
Level dataWhile you take part in the level system, or until you ask for deletion.
Ticket recordsUntil the ticket channel is deleted.
Account link cacheRefreshed after 7 days, and deleted on request.
Anti-cheat case history60 days. Records tied to an active game ban are kept until the ban or its appeal is resolved.
Backups14 days, then deleted automatically.

Your choices

  • Stop the bot from reading your messages with /level optout.
  • Ask what we store about you, or ask us to delete it, by opening a support ticket in our Discord server or by email. We handle requests within 7 days.
  • Appeal a game ban through a support ticket.

Younger users

Discord requires users to be at least 13, or older where local law says so. The bot does not ask for your age, real name, email or location, and stores nothing beyond what is listed above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top, and announce important changes in our Discord server.
